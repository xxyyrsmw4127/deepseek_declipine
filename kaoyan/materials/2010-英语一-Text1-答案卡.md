## 三、句子级核对表（自己做完精翻后逐条对）

| # | 原文锚点 | 要核的点 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Of all the changes … perhaps the most far-reaching has been the inexorable decline in the scope and seriousness of their arts coverage` | ①**不是**「消失」，是「范围与严肃性的持续下滑」；②`their` 指 → `English-language newspapers`；③`inexorable` = 不可阻挡的 |
| 2 | `It is … difficult to the point of impossibility for the average reader under the age of forty to imagine a time when …` | 形式主语 `It` 的真主语是 `to imagine a time…`；`to the point of impossibility` = 到了不可能的程度 |
| 3 | `To read such books today is to marvel at the fact that their learned contents were once deemed suitable for publication in general-circulation dailies` | ①`marvel at` = 惊叹（**不是** doubt，这是 21 题 D 项陷阱）；②`their learned contents` 的 `their` 指 `such books`；③`were once deemed` = 曾经被认为 |
| 4 | `even those reviewers who wore their learning lightly, like George Bernard Shaw and Ernest Newman` | `wear one's learning lightly` = 不炫耀学问；两人是**例子**，不影响主干 |
| 5 | `So few authors have brains enough or literary gift enough to keep their own end up in journalism … that I am tempted to define …` | `so … that` 结构；`keep one's end up` = 站得住脚、不落人后 |
| 6 | `his vast body of writings on music is unknown save to specialists` | `save` 在此是**介词** = except（不是动词「拯救」）—— 高频陷阱 |
| 7 | `postmodern readers have little use for the richly upholstered Vicwardian prose in which he specialized` | `have little use for` = 用不上/不感兴趣 → 24 题 A 的 `not appeal to`；`in which he specialized` 定语从句修饰 prose |
| 8 | `the amateur tradition in music criticism has been in headlong retreat` | `headlong retreat` = 一路溃退 |

## 三点五、24 题错误链条（你的实际答案 D，不是 B）

| 环节 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 你选 | **D** `His writings fail to follow the amateur tradition` |
| 正确 | **A** `His music criticism may not appeal to readers today` |
| 你的错因（9/30 自述） | 长难句结构未识别 → 文章大半没看懂 → 凭印象选 |
| 结构诊断 | D 是**末句移花接木**：原文末句是 `the amateur tradition in music criticism has been in headlong retreat`（业余传统在退却）。D 把「传统退却」偷换成「Cardus 的作品没有遵循该传统」——**主客颠倒**：不是 Cardus 没跟随传统，而是传统本身在死。 |
| 与 21 题同源 | 21 题 D 项用 `doubt`，原文是 `marvel at`（惊叹）—— **同一类陷阱：动词被换成近义或反义**。两题都栽在「实词精度」上，不是「没读懂大意」。 |
| 结论 | 你的瓶颈**不是阅读速度**（27 min 慢 9 min 是结果不是原因），是**句内实词与指代精度**。故精翻必须逐句写中文，不许跳句、不许意译。 |
